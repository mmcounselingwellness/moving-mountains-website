Meet Maggie

Maggie Foster, LICSW

Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker/Therapist

Accepting new clients

Growing into yourself is hard work, and honestly? It can get a bit weird. You're supposed to know what you want, who you are, which relationships are worth keeping, and somehow be an adult - all while carrying everything that came before. Maybe you're questioning yourself, struggling in relationships, feeling stuck between who you were and who you're becoming, or realizing that parts of your past still have a hold on you. Here, there's room to slow down, get curious, and find your way forward.

Hello! I'm Maggie, an LICSW with six years of experience in outpatient therapy, psychiatric care, and several years in clinical leadership. My experience ranges from complex trauma to everyday stressors. I take an approach that is rooted in attachment, trauma, and psychodynamic therapy. I know the clinical stuff - but therapy with me will always feel human.

In sessions, you can expect warmth, curiosity, honesty, and plenty of room for humor. We'll get curious about what has shaped you - and what still fits. Bring the complicated family stuff, the relationship you can't quite make sense of, or the story you've never known how to tell. You don't have to make it neat before you bring it here.

  • Attachment-Based Therapy
