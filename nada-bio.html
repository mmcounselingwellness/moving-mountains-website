Meet Nada

Nada Plooster, LICSW

Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker/Therapist

Accepting new clients

You are often doing the best you can with what you have, and sometimes it still doesn't feel like enough. You might be carrying burdens that you feel you can't set down or feeling trapped in patterns you have tried everything to stop. You might be wrestling with problems you don't know how to solve. You might have symptoms that impact your relationships, your mood, and your health in ways that are not sustainable.

I view these burdens and symptoms from the lens of how our brainbody (yes, I like to make up new words) processes information, stores experiences, and often traps the associated extreme beliefs, emotions and sensations in unhelpful ways.

By welcoming compassion, humor, and a high regard for cultural humility, I invite you to be a collaborative and active participant in therapy from determining goals and offering feedback, to taking accessible next right steps. At your own pace, we will work and practice together to restore the power and capacity to choose, feel, and live.

I provide individual therapy to adults, children, and teens carrying the heavy burdens associated with unresolved trauma, attachment deficits, systemic oppression and racism, childhood abuse or neglect, spiritual crises, relationship ruptures, grief, infertility, eating disorders, and behavioral problems.

I offer Trauma-Sensitive Yoga (TCTSY), Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Internal Family Systems (IFS) and adjunct approaches to support this journey of healing and empowerment.

When I am not wearing my therapist hat, you can find me taking life one moment at a time, trying to savor the joy with my family, the Earth, and spiritual practices. I weep, I laugh, and I move my body - almost everyday.
